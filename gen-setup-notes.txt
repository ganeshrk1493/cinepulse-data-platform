3. Step-by-Step Execution Guide
Step A: Authenticate Your Machine to GCP
Ensure your local terminal has GCP credentials ready for Terraform:

DOS
gcloud auth application-default login
(This opens a browser window. Log in with your GCP account and click Allow.)

Step B: Initialize Terraform
Run init to download the Google provider plugins:

DOS
terraform init
Expected Output: Terraform has been successfully initialized!

Step C: Preview the Plan
Run plan to verify what will be created without touching GCP yet:

DOS
terraform plan
Expected Output: Plan: 5 to add, 0 to change, 0 to destroy. (1 GCS Bucket, 1 Pub/Sub Topic, 1 Subscription, 3 BigQuery Datasets).

Step D: Apply & Provision
Deploy the infrastructure to your GCP project:

DOS
terraform apply
